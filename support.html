Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start tracking a round?

Sign in, tap to start a new round, search for your course, and log each hole as you play — score, fairways, greens, and putts. You can add notes and a scorecard photo along the way.

What is Eagle Eye Pro?

Eagle Eye Pro unlocks the full experience, including starting and resuming unlimited rounds and editing your bag. It is available as a monthly or annual subscription through the App Store.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. On your device, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, select Eagle Eye, and choose to change or cancel. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

How do I restore a subscription on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to purchase, then use the "Restore Purchases" option in the app to reactivate Eagle Eye Pro.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email brisighelladigital@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and we will delete your account and all associated data.
